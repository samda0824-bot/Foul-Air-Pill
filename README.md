# Foul-Air-Pill
A compact, portable gas detector for miscellaneous purposes. It monitors air for hazards such as CO₂ and natural gas and provides alerts when dangerous concentrations are detected.

**#Parts List**


**Compatible Sensors**


	The dual circular  Sensor space can be used by any standard circular sensor with a diameter equal or less than 0.75in some common examples are the MQ series.
	MQ-2; Combustible gases and smoke
	MQ-3; Alcohol vapor
	MQ-4; Methane and natural gas
	MQ-5; LPG and natural gas
	MQ-6; LPG, butane, and propane
	MQ-7; Carbon monoxide
	MQ-8; Hydrogen gas
	MQ-9; Carbon monoxide and flammables
	MQ-135; Harmful air pollutants

	
**Square Sensor**


	For the central Square sensor it must be less than or equal to a (0.75in x 1.25in) some common examples below. 
	MQ Breakout Board; Combustible gases and smoke
	Flat Inductive Sensor; Nearby metallic objects
	Mini PIR Sensor; Human or animal motion
	ToF Distance Module; Precise object distance
	AHT20 Module; Temperature and humidity
	BME280 Module; Temp, humidity, and pressure

	
**Microcntroller**


	ESP 32 

	
**Screen Options:**


	Whole board must be within the 5in x 2.5 in size
	16x2 Character LCD; Text and numeric readouts
	16x2 Character OLED; High-contrast text and characters
	3-inch 128x64 OLED; Graphics, text, and icons
	128x32 Graphic Display; Compact lines of custom text/data

	
**Power**


	VILROS AA Battery Holder With Micro USB Connector
